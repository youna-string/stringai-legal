This page explains how to request deletion of your String AI account and the personal data associated with it. String AI is an AI practice app for violin, viola, and cello developed by Youna Choi.

Option A — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the String AI app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Privacy & Data.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and personal data are permanently deleted within 30 days.

Option B — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email stringaiapp@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my String AI account." We verify the request and complete deletion within 30 days.

Parents of a student under 14: you do not need your child’s device. Email us from the parent email address you used to give consent, include your child’s display name, and we will verify the request and delete the account. You can also withdraw consent with the link in the consent confirmation email; that stops collection and deletes the practice records saved so far, while the account itself remains for 12 months — if you do not consent again within that time, the account is deleted too.

What is not deleted by account deletion

  • Videos you saved to your photo library — they belong to your photo library; delete them in your photo app.
  • Data in the app on another device — removed when you delete the app on that device.
  • A teacher’s own score markings for a piece — they belong to the teacher’s account (notes written about you are deleted).
  • KWS AgeGraph — if a parent completed adult verification with KWS, KWS keeps its own record under its policy; request removal through the opt-out link in the KWS confirmation email or KWS’s privacy contact.
  • Crash reports — kept by Sentry for up to 90 days; they carry a random installation identifier, not your account ID or email.
  • Copies in our operator mailbox — what was written in piece requests sent before October 25, 2026 and in-app feedback sent before October 12, 2026 was also emailed to our operator mailbox (Gmail); since then we receive only a notice, without what you wrote. We will delete all of these email copies by November 24, 2026, and right away if you ask by email.
  • Subscription payment and transaction records — Apple or Google, which processed the payment, keep your payment details and history. If you made a store purchase, we keep only that transaction record, without your account details, for 5 years after the subscription period ends (or the deletion date, if later), as Korean e-commerce law requires for payment records, and then delete it. It is used only for legally required verification and disputes.
  • Fully anonymized aggregate statistics that can no longer be linked to you.

Deletion is completed within 30 days of a verified request. See our Privacy Policy (sections 6 and 7) for details.

계정 삭제로 지워지지 않는 것

  • 사진첩에 저장한 영상 — 사진첩의 것이므로 사진 앱에서 직접 지워 주세요.
  • 다른 기기의 앱에 남은 데이터 — 그 기기에서 앱을 지우면 함께 지워집니다.
  • 선생님 본인의 곡별 악보 필기 — 선생님 계정의 것입니다(나에 대해 쓰인 메모는 삭제됩니다).
  • KWS AgeGraph — 보호자가 KWS 성인 확인을 마쳤다면 KWS 가 자체 방침에 따라 기록을 보관합니다. KWS 확인 메일의 탈퇴 링크나 KWS 개인정보 문의로 삭제를 요청할 수 있습니다.
  • 오류 보고서 — Sentry 가 최대 90일 보관하며, 계정 ID·이메일이 아닌 무작위 설치 식별자만 붙어 있습니다.
  • 운영자 메일함의 사본 — 2026년 10월 25일 전에 보낸 곡 요청과 2026년 10월 12일 전에 보낸 앱 안 의견은 적은 내용이 운영자 메일함(Gmail)에도 메일로 전달됐고, 그 뒤로는 적은 내용 없이 알림만 받습니다. 이 메일 사본은 2026년 11월 24일까지 모두 지우며, 메일로 요청하시면 바로 지웁니다.
  • 구독 결제·거래 기록 — 결제를 처리한 Apple·Google 이 결제 수단과 결제 내역을 보관합니다. 스토어 결제가 있었다면 저희는 그 거래 기록만 계정 정보 없이 이용 기간이 끝난 날(삭제한 날이 더 늦으면 그날)부터 5년 보관한 뒤 삭제합니다(전자상거래법 — 대금 결제 기록). 법이 요구하는 확인과 분쟁 대응에만 씁니다.
  • 더 이상 본인과 연결할 수 없는 완전 익명화된 집계 통계.
